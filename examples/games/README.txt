Complete game studies
=====================

Games combine input, animation, collision, scoring, sound, and the virtual
Amiga chipset. Read them after the focused graphics and audio lessons.

  pacmaze.py   Maze movement, directional animation, power mode, and AI
  defender.py  Scrolling terrain, sprites, projectiles, and sound
  raiders.py   Arcade waves, collision, scoring, and demo play
  invaders.py  Formation AI, shields, graphics, audio, and chipset features

Every game shows its active controls, supports Esc to return to the desktop,
and has a self-playing demo key for repeatable study and performance testing.
Use View > Source (or F4) to open its live source pane.
