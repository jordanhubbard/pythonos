Snake image gallery
===================

Open Image Viewer from the dock or Apps menu. It starts in this directory, so
double-click any image to see a useful result immediately.

  green-tree-python.png       Realistic natural-history photography
  retro-garden-snake.png      Colorful home-computer pixel art
  natural-history-snakes.png  Vintage watercolor study plate

The images are original, license-safe teaching assets generated for PythonOS.
They are 512 x 341 PNG files: large enough to study image decoding and display,
but deliberately compact enough for the boot image and pure-Python decoder.
